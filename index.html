What is grafverse?

grafverse is a first-person creative sandbox set on a dead moon. You conjure solids, paint them by hand, and what you paint becomes yours — recorded on-chain with the Block Media Format (BMF), a tiny open standard for composable media. Free models composite live in your browser; your owned paint stays a reference on-chain, so a shared world can never pirate paid work.

No accounts, no engine to download, no platform taking a cut. You explore free, you build, and you keep. Owned, not claimed.

How it works

🎨

Paint

Spawn in the void, conjure shapes, and spray a dead grey moon into living colour — first-person, in the browser.

🔗

Own

What you paint is recorded on-chain as a Block Media Format atom. Not a claim on a server — a thing you actually hold.

🌍

Share

Save a world or a single creation to a link anyone can walk into, free — remix it, and own what you add.
