## The grant

The creator grants everyone, permanently and irrevocably:

**1. Replicate & reuse — only through the covenant token.**
You may copy, fork, remix, modify, and build this work into your own worlds and creations — **no permission required** — by **replicating its covenant token and holding it.** That is the only permitted path. Using the work in any other way — extracting or copying it *without* replicating and holding the covenant token — is **strictly forbidden.**

**2. The royalty is automatic.**
Each on-chain replication of **this work** pays **this work's creator** their royalty, enforced by the covenant at the token layer — not by contracts or courts. You cannot replicate it *without* paying; that is the point. This covenant covers this work alone: where a creation is built from, or bundles, several works, **each one carries its own covenant and pays its own creator individually.**

**3. Provenance stays intact.**
Every replication keeps the reference to the source atom(s), so **each contributing work's royalty routes to its own creator**. You may not strip, hide, or forge provenance.

**4. Own the covenant token to use it.**
Using this work in a world means holding its **covenant token** — your owned on-chain copy. Acquiring one is the act that pays the royalty. You may **resell or replicate your covenant token** to anyone, permissionlessly, **at the covenant's fixed price**. Both reselling and replication pay the **creator's royalty** automatically via the covenant, along with the **reseller's reward** (the holder's share for passing the work on). Both amounts are set by the creator at the moment of minting, baked into the covenant, and are **at least 1 sat each.**

**5. Fixed forever by the covenant.**
Everything about this work — these terms, the creator's royalty, the reseller's reward, and the resale **price** — is fixed at the moment of minting and enforced by the covenant. None of it can be changed or stripped by a holder, a reseller, or any platform. The only way to change the price or the terms is for the **creator** to mint a **new version** of the work with their own key — something no one else can do. A new version is a **separate work with its own terms**; it never alters copies already minted, which keep their price and terms forever.

**6. As-is.**
The work is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind.

## How it works in practice

- **You find a solid you like** in someone's shared world → you replicate it → the covenant pays its creator their royalty (and the holder who passed it on their reward) ≥1 sat each. It's now yours to use, move, resell, or build on.
- **You modify it and re-mint** → the new copy references the original → its creator still earns on that lineage, and now you earn on *your* version too.
- **You leave it unchanged** → no new transaction, no new payment — you already own it.

No lawyers. No platform. No landlord. The chain does the enforcing.
