What you are looking at. The board is a covenant — a program locked into a Bitcoin output. A move spends it and creates the next board, and the script refuses anything that breaks the rules: a taken square, a move out of turn, a move after somebody has won. There is no key anywhere in it, so anyone can play and nobody can be locked out. Both browsers are watching the same chain, which is why you both see the same board without a server between you.
