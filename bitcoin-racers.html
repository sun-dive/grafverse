What just happened

Each line above is a transaction whose input was executed by Spend, the interpreter from @bsv/sdk — the same one a node uses to decide whether a spend is valid. A ✓ means the covenant accepted that move: the phase advanced legally, the driver's signature checked out, nLockTime cleared the tree, and the state written into the next output is exactly the one the script computed for itself. Anything else and the line would be red and the race would stop there.

The covenant is — of Bitcoin Script. It carries its own state in its own locking script, so each transaction is that script twice over — once in the output and once inside the preimage that authorises the spend. That is what a move costs.

Colophon. The covenant is mint/src/shell.ts. It was designed and written on 14 August 2026 by sun-dive, who made every design decision on this page — the drag race that deleted the track problem, the finish line as a UTXO so winning and being paid are one act, the three ways to lose grip told apart by cause, and the regulations themselves, felt out on the bench rather than chosen on paper — with Claude (Opus 5), who wrote the Script and the tests.

Built in six steps, each proved through the same interpreter a node runs before the next began. The mistakes are in the commit history deliberately: a fee that would have been unmineable, a covenant that enforced a sequence and loaded nothing, a test comparing a thing to itself, and depths written down instead of derived. Every one was found by measuring something rather than by reasoning about it, which is the only method here that has ever worked.
